The Care Protocol

Setting the highest standard for bedside assistance.

Hospital stays can be overwhelming for families managing jobs, children, and emotional distress. CareTaker replaces anxiety with structured, clinical-grade companionship.

01

Clinical Ward Triage

Our nursing supervisor speaks with the family to assess mobility level, post-surgical precautions, ward restrictions, and shifts needed.

02

Attendant Credentialing

We assign a dedicated caregiver from our vetted pool—trained in aseptic hygiene, wheelchair handling, bedside dignity, and patient safety.

03

Digital Family Updates

Receive shift check-in notifications, vitals logs, and doctor round summaries directly via WhatsApp so family members can rest with complete confidence.

04

Guaranteed Continuity

In the rare event an attendant falls ill or has a personal conflict, our reserve coordination network deploys a qualified replacement within 2 hours.

All CareTaker attendants undergo rigorous background verification, police clearance, and medical health screening prior to hospital deployment.
